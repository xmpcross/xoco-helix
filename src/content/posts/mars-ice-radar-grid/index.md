The next useful Mars map may not be the prettiest one. It may be the one that tells engineers where to dig.

## Several signals, one grid

Orbital radar can suggest subsurface layers, while thermal behavior hints at what lies close to the surface. Terrain maps add slopes, rocks, and landing risk.

## The usable ice problem

Ice that is scientifically interesting may be too deep, too hazardous, or too far from other mission priorities. A planning grid makes tradeoffs visible.

## Ground truth still matters

Only landed measurements can confirm what orbital data imply. The maps are strongest when treated as hypotheses for future fieldwork.
