The most delicate part of an asteroid sample may begin after landing.

## A chain of custody

Every tool, glove, container, and gas environment becomes part of the scientific record. Teams document what touched the sample because organic molecules are common on Earth.

## Why untouched grains matter

Meteorites carry valuable information, but they pass through the atmosphere and sit in terrestrial conditions before collection. Returned samples preserve smaller clues about water, heat, and early chemistry.

## Chemistry with caveats

Finding organics is not the same as finding life. The work is about tracing chemical pathways available in the early solar system.
