A stationary battery does not need to fit beneath a car floor. That changes which tradeoffs matter.

## Why sodium is interesting

Sodium is abundant and can support cell chemistries that avoid some materials used in common lithium-ion batteries. The cost advantage is not automatic, because manufacturing yield and scale matter as much as raw materials.

## The pilot is testing a system

Operators measure round-trip efficiency, temperature behavior, degradation, availability, and response to dispatch commands. A cell that performs well in a laboratory can still lose value through weak controls or excessive auxiliary power.

## Matching chemistry to service

Daily solar shifting, frequency response, and backup capacity impose different cycling patterns. The useful question is not whether sodium-ion is universally better, but where its characteristics align with a specific grid service.
