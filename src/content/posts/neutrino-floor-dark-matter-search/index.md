Dark matter detectors are designed to be quiet. Eventually they become quiet enough to hear something else.

## The background that remains

Shielding and underground sites reduce cosmic rays and radioactivity. Neutrinos still pass through almost everything, and occasionally they interact.

## Not a wall

The "neutrino floor" is not a point where experiments stop. It is a region where additional information becomes necessary.

## Designing for ambiguity

Future detectors may use target materials, timing, and direction to decide whether an event looks more like dark matter or a known neutrino source.
