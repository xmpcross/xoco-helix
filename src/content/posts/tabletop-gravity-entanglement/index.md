Gravity is easy to feel and hard to isolate.

## Shrinking the source

Tabletop experiments move small masses near sensitive probes and ask whether the measured response matches known physics.

## The false-signal problem

Electrostatic forces, vibration, temperature drift, and magnetic fields can all masquerade as tiny gravitational effects.

## Why the work matters

The experiments are modest in size but ambitious in implication. They probe where quantum control and gravity might finally meet in the same apparatus.
