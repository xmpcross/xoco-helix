The promise of portable ultrasound is not that every clinic becomes a radiology department. It is that some patients may be routed sooner.

## Help at the probe

Assisted systems can suggest angle changes, flag blurry views, and prompt repeat captures. That makes training and supervision part of the product.

## The triage test

The key question is whether the result changes care safely: faster transfer, faster reassurance, or better prioritization.

## Automation with a handoff

In low-resource settings, the strongest designs make the next step visible. A warning without a referral path is only half a tool.
