The ruins look still. The sediment does not. Layer by layer, reservoir cores can record flood pulses, neglect, dredging, and changes in land use around an ancient city.

## Water as Evidence

Ancient infrastructure is sometimes described as adaptation, but that word can flatten politics. Who maintained the reservoirs? Who received water first? Which neighborhoods were abandoned?

## The New Toolkit

Drone photogrammetry can reveal subtle channels and basins. Sediment chemistry can indicate erosion and organic input. Old excavation notes can anchor new maps to known structures.

## The Survival Story

The point is not that ancient cities solved climate stress. It is that infrastructure shaped which stresses became crises.
