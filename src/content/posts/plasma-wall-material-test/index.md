The edge of a fusion plasma is where elegant equations meet hardware.

## Testing the boundary

Candidate wall materials are bombarded, heated, cooled, and inspected for cracking, erosion, and trapped fuel.

## More than melting point

Survival is not only about enduring peak temperature. Materials must handle repeated cycles and avoid contaminating the plasma.

## Maintenance as physics

A reactor wall that performs well but cannot be replaced safely is not a complete solution. Engineering access becomes part of the experiment.
