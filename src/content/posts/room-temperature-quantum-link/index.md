Quantum networks often sound as if they belong in chilled laboratories. Some teams are trying to move part of the work onto warmer, smaller chips.

## Photons as carriers

Light is a natural messenger for quantum information because it can travel through fiber and free space. The challenge is preserving fragile correlations.

## Warm does not mean easy

Operating at room temperature removes one engineering burden and exposes others: drift, loss, fabrication variation, and detector noise.

## Scaling the claim

The next question is whether a link can move from a convincing demonstration to a repeatable network component.
