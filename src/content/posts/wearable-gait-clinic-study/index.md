A patient may walk well for six meters in a clinic and struggle for the rest of the week at home.

## More days, messier data

Wearables record steps, pace, pauses, and asymmetry across ordinary routines. That wider window can make recovery visible earlier.

## What counts as improvement

The sensor output has to connect with outcomes that patients and clinicians value: fewer falls, less fatigue, or more independence.

## Equity in the signal

Battery life, comfort, phone access, and household routines all shape the data. Trials must treat missing data as a design issue, not an afterthought.
